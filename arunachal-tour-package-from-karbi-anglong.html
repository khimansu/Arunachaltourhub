Arunachal tour package from Karbi Anglong

Karbi Anglong is in the Central Assam / Hills division of Assam, headquartered at Diphu. Here's the realistic route from there into Arunachal Pradesh, and which of our trips make sense given the distance.

Assam's largest district by area, a hill region east of Guwahati on the road towards Dima Hasao.

Getting to Arunachal from Karbi Anglong

Most journeys from Karbi Anglong district route via Guwahati, then east to the Bhalukpong gate for Tawang, or east to Itanagar for Ziro. From Diphu, expect roughly 5–6 hours of driving to reach Guwahati, the main hub for onward travel into Arunachal — treat this as an approximate range and confirm exact timing with us once we know your exact starting point in the district.

These are planning estimates, not fixed timings. Road conditions on Assam's district roads vary with the season, and the figure above assumes a reasonable highway connection to Guwahati. Tell us your exact town or village when you enquire and we will give you a real number.

Trips that suit the distance from Karbi Anglong

Because of the drive already involved in reaching Guwahati, we usually suggest a trip of five days or more from Karbi Anglong district — anything shorter spends a disproportionate share of your time getting to and from the state.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Karbi Anglong from Guwahati?

Approximately 5–6 hours by road, depending on the exact town within the district and road conditions. Guwahati is the main hub for onward travel into Arunachal Pradesh.

Do I need a permit travelling from Karbi Anglong?

Yes, unless you are a resident of Arunachal Pradesh. We arrange the Inner Line Permit for you once you send a scan of your photo ID.

Which Arunachal trip suits someone from Karbi Anglong?

Given the distance already involved in reaching Guwahati, a trip of five days or more makes the most sense. See our full package list or tell us your dates and we will suggest one.
