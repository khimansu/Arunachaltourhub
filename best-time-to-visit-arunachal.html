Best time to visit Arunachal Pradesh: tour season by month

Short answer: October to April for Tawang and snow, March to May for flowers and the eastern valleys, and avoid late June to early September everywhere.

Arunachal does not have one season, because it does not have one climate. Pasighat at 500 ft is subtropical. Tawang at 10,000 ft has hard winters. Picking the right month depends far more on where you are going than on some general idea of the best time to visit.

The Arunachal tour season, and three months people ask about

The main Arunachal tour season runs from October to April in the west, with March, April and November the sweet spot. If you are asking for the Arunachal travel best time in one line, that is it. The right Arunachal tour time for you depends on what you want to see, and three months come up most often.

Arunachal tour in October

Clear skies after the monsoon, open passes and the Tawang Festival. It is also the busiest month, because the Durga Puja holidays send travellers up from Kolkata. Rooms in Tawang go months ahead, so book by June.

Arunachal tour in December

Snow on Sela Pass and cold nights in Tawang. It is the month for snow, and the Sela Tunnel means a snowfall no longer cuts you off. Bum La and Madhuri Lake may be closed, and the Siang River Festival runs at Pasighat and Aalo. See our notes on doing Tawang in winter.

Arunachal tour in May

Warm days in the west with some pre-monsoon showers, good for Tawang and Dirang but humid in the valleys of the east. Build in a spare day, because showers become more frequent as the month goes on and roads can slip.

If you want snow

December to mid-March on Sela Pass and in Tawang town. Since the Sela Tunnel opened in 2024, a snowfall no longer means being cut off — traffic passes underneath the worst of it. Mayudia Pass near Roing is the eastern alternative, usually holding snow from late December to February. Bum La and Madhuri Lake are frequently closed in deep winter, so if those are essential, travel in April or October instead.

If you want rhododendrons

Mid-March to late April on the Sela and Bomdila slopes. Arunachal has one of the richest rhododendron communities in India, and the higher stretches turn red and pink for about six weeks. It coincides with mild days and open passes, which makes April arguably the best single month in the state.

Why the monsoon is a genuine problem

This is not ordinary Indian monsoon inconvenience. The hillsides between Tenga and Bomdila, and on the Aalo–Mechuka road, are prone to slides that close the road entirely. A closure can last hours or several days, and there is no alternative route. We do run trips in June and September, but only with padded itineraries and a clear conversation about the risk. In July and August we usually advise people to postpone.

When it is busiest

Tawang fills up during Durga Puja and the October holidays, over Christmas and New Year, and through the summer school break for travellers coming up from Assam and West Bengal. Ziro sells out entirely during the music festival weekend — if you want those dates, book four to five months ahead. March, April and November are the sweet spot: good weather, open passes and rooms available.

Frequently asked questions

Is October a good month for an Arunachal tour?

One of the best. The monsoon has cleared, the air is clean, Sela Pass is open, and the Tawang Festival usually falls this month. Book early — it coincides with the Durga Puja holidays and Tawang fills up.

Can I visit Arunachal in December?

Yes, and it is the most reliable month for snow in Tawang. Nights drop well below freezing, some hotels have limited heating, and Bum La may be closed. Pack seriously warm clothes and expect short daylight.

Is May a good time to visit Arunachal?

Reasonable in the west — Tawang and Dirang are mild and the passes are open. The lower eastern valleys around Pasighat get hot and humid, and pre-monsoon showers begin. Travel in the first half of the month rather than the second.

What is the coldest month in Tawang?

January. Daytime highs in Tawang hover near freezing and nights fall well below it, with Sela Pass considerably colder.

What is the best time for an Arunachal tour?

October to April in the west, with March, April and November the best balance of clear weather, open passes and available rooms. Avoid July and August.

Is May a good time for an Arunachal tour?

It works for Tawang and Dirang, with warm days and occasional pre-monsoon showers. Keep a spare day in the itinerary and avoid the east, which is hot and humid.
