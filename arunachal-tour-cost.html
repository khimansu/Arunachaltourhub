Arunachal tour package price: what a trip really costs

Per-day budgets, package prices and the costs most quotes leave out. All figures are indicative for 2026 and assume twin sharing.

The honest answer is ₹2,800 to ₹4,200 per person per day for land arrangements, plus flights. Everything below explains where that number comes from and what moves it.

Arunachal tour budget in one paragraph

For a first Arunachal tour budget, plan on ₹22,900 per person for the seven-day Tawang trip when four people share a vehicle, land arrangements only. That is the Arunachal tour package price from Guwahati. From Kolkata, add a return flight of ₹5,500–9,000, so the total travel cost is roughly ₹28,400–31,900 per person. A couple travelling alone pays ₹31,000–34,000 for the land portion, or about ₹36,500–43,000 with the flight from Kolkata.

These are indicative figures and change with season and group size. For a firm number, use the Guwahati price table or the Kolkata price breakdown.

Five honest ways to spend less

  1. Travel in a group of four or six. This is worth more than every other saving combined.
  2. Go in March, April or November rather than October or over New Year. Same weather, noticeably lower hotel rates.
  3. Choose homestays over hotels in Ziro and the Siang valley. Cheaper, and honestly better.
  4. Skip Bum La if the budget is tight. It adds several thousand rupees and is frequently closed by weather anyway.
  5. Fly into Guwahati on the first flight of the day. You gain half a driving day, which can remove a whole night's hotel cost.

A note on very cheap quotes: if someone quotes a seven-day Tawang trip at ₹12,000 per person, something is missing — usually it is a shared vehicle you will be sharing with strangers, hotels booked on arrival rather than in advance, or a per-person price that assumes a full ten-seater. Ask for the vehicle type, the group size assumed, and the hotel names before comparing.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 7-day Arunachal trip cost?

Around ₹22,900 per person for land arrangements on twin sharing in a group of four, plus flights. Two travellers alone pay closer to ₹32,000 each; a group of six pays about ₹20,000.

Is Arunachal expensive compared to Himachal or Uttarakhand?

Per day, it is similar or slightly higher, mainly because distances are longer and fuel and supplies cost more to get into the hills. The bigger difference is the flight — reaching Guwahati costs more from most Indian cities than reaching Delhi or Chandigarh.

Can I do Arunachal on a budget of Rs 15,000?

For a five-day trip to Dirang and Bomdila from Guwahati, yes, if you travel in a group of four or more and stay in guest houses. A Tawang trip within that budget is only realistic with shared taxis and a flexible schedule.

Are there hidden costs in tour packages?

There shouldn't be. Ask any operator to confirm in writing whether the price includes the permit, the Bum La vehicle, monument fees and all meals. Those four items are where most quotes differ.
