Frequently asked questions

Is the trip planner accurate?

It's a starting point, not a substitute for talking to us. It narrows seven packages down to the one or two that best fit your days, interests and group — then we refine it once we know your actual dates.

Does the planner book anything automatically?

No. It only recommends. Booking happens through the booking page or WhatsApp, after you've seen a full itinerary and price.

What if I have unusual dates or a very specific request?

Skip the planner and go straight to the contact form — tell us what you have in mind and we'll build around it.
