Guwahati to Arunachal tour: plan, travel and price

The road from Guwahati into Arunachal: how long it takes, what a tour plan looks like day by day, what it costs and who to book it with.

Guwahati is the gateway to Arunachal Pradesh. Every western and central route begins here, so a Guwahati to Arunachal tour comes down to three decisions: which road, how many days, and who arranges it.

Guwahati to Arunachal travel: the three routes

RouteRoadTimeBest for
WestGuwahati → Bhalukpong → Dirang → Tawang, ~500 km2 days, never 1Tawang & Sela Pass
CentralGuwahati → Itanagar (320 km) → Ziro (110 km)10–12 hours of drivingZiro Valley
EastStart from Dibrugarh, not Guwahati—Mechuka & Siang valley

The full distance and drive-time table is on the Arunachal travel map page. The one rule: do not try Guwahati to Tawang in a single day. It is unsafe, it ignores altitude, and it is why we always overnight at Bhalukpong, Nameri or Dirang.

Guwahati to Arunachal tour plan: 7 days to Tawang

DayPlan
1Guwahati to Bhalukpong or Nameri (230 km, about 5 hours)
2Bhalukpong to Dirang (140 km, 5–6 hours)
3Dirang and Sangti, then over Sela Pass to Tawang (135 km)
4Tawang Monastery and around
5Bum La Pass and Madhuri Lake (extra permit)
6Tawang back to Dirang or Bomdila
7Return to Guwahati

Short of time? The 5-day Dirang loop and a long weekend to Sangti Valley are both realistic from Guwahati. Longer plans are on the itinerary page.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Tawang from Guwahati?

About 500 km by road, planned as two days with a night at Dirang or Bomdila.

Can I do a Guwahati to Arunachal tour over a long weekend?

Not to Tawang, which needs seven days. A long weekend to Dirang and Sangti Valley is realistic, and a five-day Ziro or Dirang trip works well on a short leave.

Do I need a permit for a tour from Guwahati?

Yes. Anyone who is not a resident of Arunachal needs an Inner Line Permit. We apply for it for you at the government fee.

Is there a cheaper way to travel from Guwahati to Arunachal?

Buses and shared taxis cost less than a hired car but are slower and only reach towns. Our blog compares them in Arunachal travel by bus and car.
