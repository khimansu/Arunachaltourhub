Ziro Valley tour package

Four nights on a plateau of terraced rice fields where the Apatani farm fish and rice in the same paddy. The gentlest introduction to Arunachal, and for many people the most memorable.

Ziro is flat, which in Arunachal is remarkable. A wide valley at 5,500 ft, ringed by pine hills, with villages of bamboo houses set among rice terraces worked the same way for centuries.

The Apatani cultural landscape is on UNESCO's tentative World Heritage list, and unlike Tawang the appeal here is not scenery you photograph from a car window — it is walking through villages, sitting in kitchens and understanding a farming system that supports an unusually dense population without machines or draught animals.

The Ziro Music Festival

Held on the valley floor in late September, this is the best known music event in the North East and the one weekend Ziro genuinely fills up. Accommodation is almost entirely camping and village homestays, and it sells out months ahead. If you want the festival, contact us in May or June — by August there is usually nothing left. Dates change each year; ask us for the current ones.

Visiting Apatani villages respectfully

Older Apatani women wear distinctive facial tattoos and nose plugs, a practice discontinued decades ago. They are photographed constantly by visitors and many are tired of it. Ask first, accept no as an answer, and if someone agrees, sit and talk rather than shooting and leaving. Our guides are from the villages and will introduce you properly, which changes the encounter entirely.

Combining Ziro with Tawang

Possible in ten days, with one long transfer via Itanagar. Ziro and Tawang are in different corridors and there is no short route between them — see the 10-day itinerary.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Ziro Music Festival?

Late September, on the valley floor. Dates are announced each year — ask us and we will confirm the current ones. Book four to five months ahead; accommodation is limited to camping and homestays.

How do I reach Ziro Valley?

Lilabari airport at North Lakhimpur is closest, about four hours away. Donyi Polo Airport at Hollongi near Itanagar and Naharlagun railway station are the other practical options. Guwahati is around 10 hours by road.

Is Ziro suitable for families?

Very. It sits at a comfortable 5,500 ft with no high passes, short driving days and walking rather than climbing. It is the trip we recommend most for families with children or older parents.

What is the best time to visit Ziro?

March to May and September to November. The rice terraces are greenest from June to August, but that is also peak monsoon with real landslide risk on the approach road.
