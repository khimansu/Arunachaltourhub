Booking your Arunachal tour

Five steps between reading this page and standing at Sela Pass. Nothing here requires you to pay before you've seen a real itinerary and a firm price.

How booking actually works

  1. 1

    Tell us your trip

    Dates (or a month, if flexible), your starting city, group size and which package interests you — use the form alongside, WhatsApp, or the contact page.

  2. 2

    We check real availability

    Hotel rooms in Tawang and Ziro are limited in season. We confirm dates against actual room availability before we quote — not after you've paid.

  3. 3

    You get a day-by-day itinerary and a firm price

    Named hotels, drive times, what's included and what isn't. Usually within a few hours. No obligation at this stage, and no charge for revising it.

  4. 4

    25% advance confirms your dates

    Once you're happy with the plan, a 25% advance locks in your rooms and vehicle. We'll send a payment link — UPI or bank transfer, no card details handled through this website.

  5. 5

    Permit, then balance, then travel

    Send a scan of your photo ID and we apply for your Inner Line Permit. The balance is due seven days before arrival. You'll have your final itinerary and driver contact before you fly.

What we need from you

  • A scan or clear photo of a government photo ID (Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence) for every traveller, for the Inner Line Permit.
  • A passport-sized photograph for each traveller.
  • Any health conditions relevant to altitude, disclosed before you travel to Tawang, Sela or Bum La.
  • Your return flight details, once booked, so we can plan the last day around them.

What you will never be asked for on this site

We do not collect card numbers or take payment through this website. Every payment request comes as a direct UPI ID or bank transfer detail from our own number, after you've already seen and agreed to a price. If anyone contacts you claiming to be us and asks for payment before you've received a written itinerary, treat it as fraud and call us directly to confirm.

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay in full to book?

No. A 25% advance confirms your dates; the remaining 75% is due seven days before you arrive.

How do I pay?

By UPI or bank transfer, using details we send you directly once you've agreed to a quote. We don't process card payments through this website.

Can I book without deciding the exact itinerary?

Yes. Send us your dates and group size and select "custom / not sure yet" — we'll come back with two or three options to choose from.

What if I need to cancel after booking?

Refund terms depend on how close to your travel date you cancel. Full detail is on the terms page — more than 30 days out, you get a full refund less permit and bank charges.

How far ahead should I book?

Four to eight weeks for most trips. For Tawang or Ziro during October, Durga Puja or the Ziro Music Festival, book four to five months ahead — those dates fill first.
