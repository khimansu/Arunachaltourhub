Frequently asked questions

How many districts does Assam have?

35, as of the current reorganisation. They're grouped into five divisions: Lower Assam, North Assam, Upper Assam, Central Assam and the Barak Valley.

Which Assam district is closest to Arunachal Pradesh?

Several districts border Arunachal directly, including Sonitpur, Biswanath, Lakhimpur, Dhemaji, Charaideo and Tinsukia, depending on which part of Arunachal you mean. Sonitpur (Tezpur) is the closest practical gateway for Tawang; Lakhimpur for Ziro; Dibrugarh and Tinsukia for the east.

Is Guwahati the only way into Arunachal from Assam?

No, though it's the main one. Dibrugarh is the better gateway for East Arunachal (Pasighat, Mechuka, Namdapha), saving around 14 hours of road transfer compared with routing through Guwahati.
